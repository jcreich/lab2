Group Scheduler

Find overlapping availability across a roster, pack in as many valid group timeslots as possible, and fill everyone's remaining hours — all in the browser, nothing to install.

Scheduling mode

Group mode packs people into shared timeslots (set your own minimum group size below — pairs, trios, whatever fits). Coverage mode assigns exactly one RA per shift and actively avoids scheduling two people at the same time — good for things like desk or lab-monitoring coverage.

Timeslot rules

These apply to every day the roster is available.

Recurring blocked meeting (optional)

A fixed commitment everyone must skip — e.g. a weekly lab meeting. Leave the day blank if there isn't one.
